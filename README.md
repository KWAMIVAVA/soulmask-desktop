## ✨ What This App Does

Soulmask Desktop is best described as a map for your game files. It does not play the game for you, and it does not alter your saves. It simply gathers the locations you care about into one readable window.

What you can do with it:

- **See your data paths.** The helper lists the folders where Soulmask keeps its local data, so you always know where to look.
- **Review your config files.** Configuration files control many small behaviours. The helper points you to them so you can open or copy them without guessing.
- **Manage export caches.** Over time, temporary exported data can pile up. The helper shows you where that cache lives so you can check its size or clear it out.
- **Find your export folders.** Anything you have exported has to land somewhere. The helper shows those destinations plainly.
- **Keep Windows and macOS tidy.** Both platforms are supported, and each one gets the correct folder locations for that system.

Everything is read-and-show by default. You stay in control of what gets opened, copied, or removed.

## 🗂️ Understanding Your Data Paths

A "data path" is simply the folder where a program keeps its files. Soulmask, like most games, stores its local data in a predictable place — but that place is buried a few levels deep and hidden by default on some systems.

Soulmask Desktop surfaces that location for you. When you see your data path in the helper, you can:

- Open it directly in your file browser.
- Copy it and paste it into any other tool or document.
- Check how much space it is using.
- Make a manual backup by copying the folder somewhere safe.

Knowing your data path is the single most useful piece of information for troubleshooting, backing up, and moving to a new computer.

---

## ⚙️ Config and Export Caches

**Config files** are the small text files that record your preferences. They are safe to read, and they are useful to copy before you change anything. The helper shows you exactly where they live so you never have to search for them.

**Export caches** are the temporary copies created while data is being exported. They are helpful during a session and unnecessary afterwards. If your drive is filling up, checking the cache size is a sensible first step. The helper shows you the cache folder so you can inspect it yourself — you decide whether to clear it.

A simple habit: before deleting anything, copy the folder to a backup location first. Storage is cheap; re-creating lost data is not.

---

## 📤 Working With Export Folders

Export folders are where finished exports end up. They can grow quickly, and they are easy to lose track of when they sit inside a deep folder tree.

With Soulmask Desktop you can:

- See the default export destination at a glance.
- Open it in one step instead of clicking through five folders.
- Confirm that a new export actually arrived where you expected.
- Keep separate destinations for different projects by noting each path.

If you share a computer, this is also the quickest way to show someone else where their exported files are stored.

No. The helper is self-contained and runs on its own.

Yes. Both are supported, and the helper picks the correct folders for the system it runs on.

That usually means Soulmask has not created that folder on this computer yet. Launch the game once, then reopen the helper and check again.

**Can I move the helper to another drive?**
Yes. It works from any normal folder. Avoid moving it while it is running.

**Is my information sent anywhere?**
No. Everything stays on your own computer.
